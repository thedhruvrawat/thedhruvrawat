### About me
I am a quantitative researcher and developer at **Nomura** in the Global Markets Division, working on architecture of low-latency pricing and analytics engines for securitized products. Prior to this, I worked on Change Data Capture (CDC) in YugabyteDB at **Yugabyte**.

Prior to joining Nomura, I completed my bachelors at <a href="https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/pilani/">BITS Pilani</a>, with a dual major in *Computer Science* and *Economics*. During that time, I was part of **Advanced Data and Parallel Technologies (ADaPT) Lab**, working with <a href="https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/pilani/jagat-sesh-challa/">Dr. Jagat Sesh Challa</a> on unsupervised machine learning algorithms and systems for tasks like clustering, classification, frequent-itemset mining and anomaly detection.

---

### Research Interests
* **ML-for-Systems:** Early detection of workload characteristic drift from design assumptions.
* **Distributed Systems & HPC:** Mechanisms for autonomous reconfiguration and low-latency control planes.
* **Systems-for-ML:** Co-designing system adaptivity with application-level execution for large-scale workloads.
